## Research interests

- **Excitons and optical properties** in low-dimensional materials and van der Waals heterostructures, using GW and the Bethe–Salpeter equation.
- **Electron–phonon interactions and carrier transport** from GW perturbation theory and the ab initio Boltzmann transport equation.
- **Computational methods and workflows**, combining many-body theory, high-performance computing, and AI-assisted calculations.
